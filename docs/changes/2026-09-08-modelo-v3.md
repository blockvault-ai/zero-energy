# Change v3 — Foundational model redesign

**Date:** 2026-09-08

## Note

The previous model distinguished "ZREC Genesis" (obtained via the Genesis Right in the Presale and in Merits) from "Energy ZREC" (mined via PoE). This created contradictions: an origin that did not mint ZDAO, a right ("Genesis Right") with a single-consumption mechanic that complicated the model, and a distribution table that did not reflect the actual conversion.

The model is simplified into three decisions:

1. **ZREC Genesis removed.** ZREC is a single fungible asset with two **accounting** origins: Merits (foundational) and Energy (PoE).
2. **Genesis Right removed.** The Merits choice between ZDAO and ZREC is a plain choice at claim time, resolved on the off-chain record.
3. **TGE defined.** Launch milestone that fixes the reference date `t0` and opens the 6-month Claim window.

Additionally: the Presale delivers ZDAO only; Merits choose between ZDAO (free) or ZREC (6-month cliff + 2 %/month, remaining in staking and producing free ZDAO).

## Rules of the new model

- ZREC is not sold in the Presale. It is obtained via:
  - Merits converted to ZREC (with vesting), or
  - Mining via PoE (Energy ZREC).
- The Presale delivers ZDAO only.
- Merits choose: ZDAO (free) or ZREC (6-month cliff + 2 %/month in staking).
- The chosen ZREC remains in staking producing ZDAO; the generated ZDAO is immediately free.
- Staking = ZREC → ZDAO (the only mechanism that mints the 500M reserve).

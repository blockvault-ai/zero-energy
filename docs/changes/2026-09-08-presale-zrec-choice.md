# Change v3.1 — Presale ZDAO/ZREC choice

**Date:** 2026-09-08

## Note

The v3 model delivered ZDAO only in the Presale. This is revised: **Presale participants may choose, at claim time, between ZDAO or ZREC**, both free of Vesting (no lock, no mandatory staking stay).

This restores a ZREC entry path for Presale buyers, but without the vesting schedule that applies to Merits. The distinction between the two branches is now:

- **Presale** → choice of ZDAO or ZREC, both **free**.
- **Merits** → choice of ZDAO (free) or ZREC (cliff 6 months + 2 %/month, remaining in staking).
